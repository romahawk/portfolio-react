# PORTFOLIO-WEBSITE-HANDOFF.md

## 0. Document purpose

This is the canonical handoff for **Roman Mazuryk’s portfolio / professional website project**.

It is designed so that a new LLM, Claude Project, coding agent, or collaborator can continue work on the site without needing access to prior ChatGPT conversations.

**Primary site:** https://www.mazuryk.dev/  
**Repository:** https://github.com/romahawk/portfolio-react

The website is not intended to be a generic developer portfolio. Its purpose is to support Roman’s transition into AI-enabled product/project/system roles while also creating a credible business-development surface for AI workflow consulting and product-building work.

---

# 1. Strategic purpose

The portfolio should accomplish four things simultaneously:

1. **Career positioning**
   - Support applications for:
     - Technical Project Manager
     - Product Manager / Product Owner
     - Systems / Implementation Project Manager
     - Solutions Consultant / Solutions Engineer
     - AI Product Builder
     - AI-enabled workflow / automation roles
   - Preserve Roman’s seniority and prior commercial/project credibility rather than position him as a junior software developer.

2. **AI positioning**
   - Demonstrate practical use of AI in product development, automation, operations, research, workflows, and software delivery.
   - Show that Roman can identify where AI creates business value, prototype solutions, and integrate AI into real processes.

3. **Business development**
   - Act as a trust layer for potential SME consulting / implementation clients.
   - Support an offer around AI workflow audits, prototype sprints, internal tools, knowledge systems, SOP automation, dashboards, and workflow redesign.

4. **Technical proof**
   - Show that Roman can build and ship software himself using modern full-stack and AI-assisted development workflows.
   - Projects should function as proof-of-work rather than as disconnected hobby projects.

### Audience

- Operations leaders

- companies exploring practical AI adoption
- potential consulting/project partners
- recruiters hiring for AI-enabled product/operations roles

AI should be applied to real workflows, measurable pain points, fragmented information, repetitive coordination, decision support, and internal operations — not just added as a chatbot.

### Potential offers

- AI Prototype Sprint
- Internal AI Tools
- Knowledge / SOP Systems
- Operations Dashboards
- AI-assisted research and decision workflows

- shipped prototypes,

- proposed changes,
